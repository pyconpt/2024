Esmerald and Lilya - How to speed up the development process
.ical
2024-10-17 12:10–12:40, Auditorium

Speeding up the development process is something that every person should be able to. With the massive amount of boilerplate needed, that can take ages. Esmerald and Lilya address that for you.

My name is Tiago Silva, a portuguese software engineer based in UK and with experience in many fields of the industry.

Having worked for big players in the market, including Bank of England, I was able to develop a fully fledged python web framework called Esmerald (https://esmerald.dev) and more recently addressing ASGI tooling by creating Lilya (https://lilya.dev) already being adopted as well.

From its inception in 2022, Esmerald helped countless organisations saving thousands of hours in development and without any kind of marketing, Lilya is now doing the same.

Both developed by Dymmond (https://dymmond.com) Esmerald came to stay and help python and non python engineers maximizing the time and minimizing the effort by reducing the human led bugs and increasing the productivity and imagination to the next level.

Audience Level: Beginner What are the main topics of your talk?:

Framework, development, optimization, data, we, API, architecture
